How lead score works
  • 🔴 Hot (70–100) — urgent signals, hospital/discharge, active tour
  • 🟡 Warm (35–69) — tour interest, memory care, follow-up set
  • ⚪ Cold (0–34) — minimal signals or early research stage
  • 🩶 Stale — no activity in 30+ days, regardless of score

Score drops -1/day after 14 days of inactivity (max -20). Negating phrases like "not urgent" cancel positive signals.
